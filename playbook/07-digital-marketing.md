# 07 — Digital Marketing Engine: Website, Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp, CRM

> **Note for Yash: how paid ads actually work** (read twice)
> 1. **Search ads (Google)** capture people who already have the need and are typing it. They bring few people, high intent and higher cost per click.
> 2. **Social ads (Meta, YouTube, LinkedIn)** *interrupt* people who match a profile. They bring many people, lower intent and lower cost per click. They work by **creating** demand, and they need great creative (video) and a strong free offer (the Snapshot).
> 3. **Retargeting** shows ads again to people who already visited your site or watched your videos. It's cheap and converts best. That's why we build the video audience first.
> 4. **The only metric that matters** is *cost per qualified lead* → *cost per site visit* → *cost per mandate*. Clicks, likes and impressions are vanity until they lead to these.
> 5. **Speed-to-lead:** call or WhatsApp every new lead **within 15 minutes** during working hours. Leads go cold within hours; this alone doubles conversion.

### 2D. Bidding plan (learning path)
- **Weeks 1–4:** "Maximise clicks" with a max CPC cap (₹80–120), to learn which keywords convert. Expect real-estate CPCs of roughly ₹50–180 in India ([benchmarks](https://apexinfluence.in/blog/google-ads-benchmarks-india)); niche B2B terms may run higher.
- **After ~30 conversions:** switch to **"Maximise conversions"**, then **Target CPA**.
- **Import offline conversions:** mark leads in the CRM as "Qualified", "Site visit" and "Mandate", and send them back to Google (via GCLID / enhanced conversions for leads). Then Google learns to find *mandates*, not form-fillers. Set this up by month 3. It's what separates amateurs from professionals.

## 3. Meta (Facebook + Instagram)

### 3A. Campaigns

| Campaign | Objective | Audience | Creative | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 — Video views (top of funnel)** | Engagement / ThruPlay | Bengaluru + Rural + North districts, 28–65, broad (let Meta find people) | Hoskote episodes + Kannada explainers | — (builds the audience) |
| **M2 — Landowner leads** | Leads (Instant Form) **and** click-to-WhatsApp (test both) | (a) Broad, Bangalore region, 28–65 (b) Interests: real estate investing, agriculture, property, land (c) Retarget: 50%+ video viewers, site visitors | Kannada + English: "Land near Hoskote? Earn monthly rent without selling" | Instant form with *higher-intent* setting + qualifying questions |
| **M3 — Retargeting** | Leads | Site visitors 30/90 days, form openers, IG/FB engagers | Testimonials, site-visit invite, "free report in 72 hours" | `/lp/snapshot` |
| **M4 — Landowner meetings** | Reach / Awareness | 5–10 km radius around the meeting village | "Landowners' meeting: Saturday, [venue], 11 am" (Kannada) | Call / WhatsApp |

### 3B. Instant-form qualifying questions (filters junk leads)
1. Where is your land? (Hoskote / Nelamangala–Dabaspete / Doddaballapur / Devanahalli / Other)
2. How many acres? (<2 / 2–5 / 5–10 / 10+)
3. Who owns it? (Me / My family / I'm a broker / Other)
4. When would you like to start? (Within 6 months / 6–12 months / Just exploring)

Leads answering <2 acres, "Other" location or "broker" get routed differently (brokers go to the partner programme).

### 3C. Creative formulas that work for this audience
- **"Neighbour proof":** drone footage of a warehouse next to farmland + "This was farmland 2 years ago. Today it earns ₹X lakh a month."
- **"Talking head in Kannada":** field BD (a local face) explains in 30 s, filmed in a field near Hoskote, with the warehouse behind.
- **"Number shock":** large text "1 acre ≈ ₹4.5–5 lakh rent/month*" + small disclaimer, followed by an honest explanation.
- **"Son's perspective" (for Ravi):** "Your parents' land in Hoskote is earning almost nothing. Here's what it could earn." English, for IT professionals.
- **Carousel:** the 7 steps.
- **Rule:** make 3–5 creatives per campaign; after 2 weeks, kill the bottom half and make new versions of the winner.

**Meta tracking:** Pixel + Conversions API on the website; the CRM sends "Qualified" and "Site visit" events back (via Zapier/Make or native integration) so Meta optimises for quality.

## 8. CRM and measurement: the company's brain

**CRM:** **Zoho CRM** (Indian, cheap, WhatsApp/telephony integrations, ~₹1–1.5k/user/month) or **HubSpot** (free starter; better marketing automation; more expensive later). Recommend **Zoho CRM + Zoho Bookings + Interakt/WATI**.

**Pipelines (three):**
1. **Landowner:** New lead → Contacted → Qualified → Snapshot sent → Site visit done → Feasibility paid → Mandate signed → Lost (reason)
2. **Occupier:** Contacted → Requirement known → Sites shared → Site visit → LOI → Lease signed
3. **Partner:** Identified → Contacted → Met → Agreement signed → Active (gave a lead) → Producing (lead converted)

**Every lead must have:** source (UTM / "Field BD" / "Broker: name"), date, owner, next action + date. **No lead without a next action.**

**Weekly dashboard (Monday 9 am, 30 min):**

| Metric | Target (steady state, from month 4) |
|---|---|
| New landowner leads (all sources) | 15–25 / week |
| % contacted within 15 min | ≥ 80% |
| Qualified conversations | 7–8 / week (30/month) |
| Snapshots sent | 2–3 / week (10/month) |
| Site visits | 1 / week (4/month) |
| Paid feasibility reports | 2 / month |
| Mandates | 1 every 1–2 months |
| Cost per qualified lead (paid) | Track; aim to bring it down every month |
| Cost per site visit | Track |
| Partner leads | ≥ 30% of qualified leads by month 6 |
| LinkedIn followers / Hoskote video views | Track trend |

**UTM rule:** every link you post anywhere uses UTMs (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`). Use Google's free Campaign URL Builder.

## 9. Testing discipline: "the marketing lab"

Every month, run **one deliberate experiment**, write it down and decide from data:
- Month 2: Kannada vs English landowner ads
- Month 3: Instant Form vs click-to-WhatsApp
- Month 4: "free report" vs "free site visit" as the offer
- Month 5: talking-head vs drone creative
- Month 6: landing page with vs without the calculator
- Month 7+: new audiences (NRI, HNI investor-owners), new channels (newspaper, radio, Kannada YouTube creators)

Log format: *Hypothesis → What we changed → Spend → Result → Decision.* This log is your marketing education.
