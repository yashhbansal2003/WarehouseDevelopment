# 02 — Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) and Exactly Where to Find Them

> **Note for Yash:** an ICP is a description so specific that your field person could walk into a village and say "that family, not that one". Vague targets ("landowners") waste ad money. Specific targets ("Vokkaliga family, 6 acres, 1 km off NH-75 near Hoskote, son works in Whitefield") make ads cheap and scripts sharp.

---

## ICP 1 — The Landowning Family (our main customer)

### 1A. Who exactly

| Attribute | Ideal | Acceptable | Reject (politely) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Land size | 4–12 acres (≈ 80k–2.6 L sq ft buildable) | 3–15 acres | <2.5 acres (too small for a modern 3PL warehouse) or >25 acres (refer to institutional buyers for a referral fee) |
| Location | Within ~5 km of NH-75 (Hoskote), NH-48 (Nelamangala–Dabaspete), NH-648 / STRR (Doddaballapur–Hoskote–Dabaspete), or SH-9/Doddaballapur Road | 5–10 km with a good approach road | Interior land with a single-lane approach road |
| Approach road | ≥ 12 m (40 ft) wide, so a 40-ft trailer can turn in | 9 m, if it can be widened | <9 m with no fix |
| Title | Clear RTC (pahani), mutation in their names, few co-owners, no litigation | Inheritance not yet partitioned but family aligned | Active litigation, govt/inam/PTCL land, BDA/BMRDA acquisition notifications |
| Zoning | Industrial / logistics zone in the Master Plan, or convertible | Agricultural but convertible | Green belt / reserved / tank bed / buffer zone |
| Money | Wants monthly income, and either has some capital or is open to borrowing | Can contribute land only (so a lease/revenue-share model like Hoskote 001 works) | Wants a lump sum *now* (send to a land buyer) |
| Decision-maker | Head of family + an educated son/daughter/son-in-law who works in Bangalore | Single owner | 8+ co-owners who don't agree |

### 1B. The four landowner personas

1. **"Appaji" — the patriarch (55–75).** Farmer or retired, Kannada-first, proud of the land, afraid of losing it or being cheated. Wants: respect, a slow process, the land staying in the family, income for the grandchildren. Trigger: neighbour's land just got a warehouse paying ₹X lakh a month. **Reach via:** field BD, village elders, temple/community meetings, the Hoskote site visit.
2. **"Ravi" — the IT son/daughter (28–45).** Works in Whitefield, ORR, Electronic City or Manyata. English + Kannada. Googles everything and watches YouTube. Has the internet but not the land; Appaji has the land but not the internet. **Ravi is the most important digital target.** Wants: a trustworthy, professional process; numbers; a lawyer-reviewed contract. **Reach via:** Google Search, Meta/YouTube ads geotargeted to Bangalore, LinkedIn posts, website.
3. **"Suresh" — the local entrepreneur-landowner (35–55).** Already runs a business (transport, quarry, real estate, hotel, petrol pump). Has seen money; wants to "do a project". May have capital. **Reach via:** local business associations, Rotary/Lions clubs in Hoskote/Nelamangala/Doddaballapur, banks, CAs.
4. **"The investor-owner" (40–65).** A Bangalore businessperson or professional who bought land on these corridors 5–15 years ago as an investment. English-first; HNI. **Reach via:** CAs, wealth advisors, private-banking RMs, LinkedIn, Google.

### 1C. Exactly where to find them

**Physical (Engine A):**

| Place | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Dishank app** (Karnataka govt, free) | Shows survey-number boundaries on a satellite map. Field BD maps every parcel ≥3 acres within 5 km of the highways. This is how we build the **"Land Bank Map" of 100 parcels** |
| **Bhoomi RTC** (landrecords.karnataka.gov.in) | Look up owner names and extent for each survey number (public record). Never cold-visit with a printout in hand, which feels like surveillance. Use it to *prepare* |
| **Sub-Registrar Offices**: Hoskote, Nelamangala, Doddaballapur, Devanahalli | Document writers ("bond writers") and stamp vendors outside know every family selling, partitioning or leasing land. Make friends; offer a referral fee |
| **Licensed land surveyors and local advocates** (title lawyers) in each taluk | They see every land document. Referral partners |
| **Local land brokers** (every village has 2–5) | Main sourcing channel; pay a referral fee on mandate signing (see file 08) |
| **Gram Panchayat members, PACS (cooperative society) secretaries, dairy cooperative heads** | Respected; can convene the 15–30 person landowner meetings |
| **Temples / community halls / Kalyana Mantapas** | Venues for landowner meetings |
| **Local banks**: Canara Bank, SBI, Karnataka Gramin Bank, DCC Bank branches in Hoskote/Nelamangala/Doddaballapur | Branch managers know who has land and big deposits; ask for an introduction, not a list |
| **Petrol pumps, dhabas, truck lay-bys on NH-75 / NH-48** | Hoarding and flex placements ("Own land near the highway? Earn monthly rent. Call…") |
| **Hoskote 001 site hoarding** | Our best ad: faces the road, with a QR code + WhatsApp number |

**Digital (Engine B):**

| Channel | Targeting |
|---|---|
| Google Search | Keywords like "lease land for warehouse", "warehouse construction on own land", "joint development agreement land Bangalore", "warehouse rent per sq ft Hoskote", "how to convert agricultural land to industrial Karnataka" |
| Meta (Facebook + Instagram) | Location: Bengaluru city + Bengaluru Rural + Bengaluru North districts; age 28–60; Kannada + English creatives; interests: real estate investment, agriculture, land, property. Lead form with qualifying questions |
| YouTube | Hoskote 001 series as in-feed and in-stream ads, Bangalore-only; also Kannada farming/real-estate channel audiences |
| WhatsApp | Click-to-WhatsApp ads + broadcast list for leads; most landowners prefer WhatsApp to forms |

## ICP 2 — Occupiers (tenants for built-to-suit)

### 2A. Who exactly

| Segment | Share of Bangalore leasing | Typical need | Decision-maker title | Why they'd choose BTS with us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3PLs** | 58% | 50k–2.5 L sq ft, mostly for a named client contract; need speed | Head – Real Estate / Infrastructure; VP Solutions Design; Regional Ops Head South; Business Development (they win the client, then need space fast) | Custom specs, location near *their client's* demand, faster than waiting for a park |
| **Manufacturers** (auto parts, electronics, EV, aerospace, engineering) | 16% | 50k–1.5 L sq ft for finished goods / inbound components / vendor park | Plant Head; GM Supply Chain; Head – Projects; CFO | Near their plant (Hoskote, Narasapura, Devanahalli ITIR, Doddaballapur, Bidadi), heavy-floor specs, longer leases |
| **E-commerce / quick commerce** | 15% | Fulfilment centres 1–3 L sq ft; mother hubs 50–100k | Real Estate / Expansion Head; Network Planning | Speed to market, specific dock and height specs |
| **FMCG + their C&F agents** | 8% | 30–100k sq ft depots; C&F agents (Carrying & Forwarding agents of HUL, ITC, Britannia, Nestlé, Dabur, Marico, pharma companies) | C&F agent owner; company's Regional Logistics Manager | C&F agents are an **overlooked niche**: owner-run, decide fast, need 30–80k sq ft, sign 5–9 year leases |
| Pharma / cold chain distributors | small but growing | 20–60k sq ft, temperature-controlled | Owner / Head Ops | Specialised build our vendors can deliver |

### 2B. Where to find them

- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** (₹~6,000/month; worth it from month 2). Search: Geography = Bengaluru; titles = "real estate", "infrastructure", "warehouse", "supply chain", "logistics", "network planning", "facilities", "projects"; industries = Logistics, Transportation, Retail, Manufacturing, Consumer Goods.
- **Registered lease data:** CRE Matrix and Propstack publish who leased where (e.g., DHL and Amazon above). Every new lease is a lead: *their next expansion*, and *their competitors'*.
- **Job postings:** a company hiring a "Warehouse Manager – Hoskote" or "Site Lead – Bangalore FC" is about to need space. Set Naukri/LinkedIn job alerts.
- **Industry associations and events:** CII Karnataka, FKCCI, Bangalore Chamber of Industry & Commerce (BCIC), KASSIA, Peenya Industries Association, **Doddaballapur Industrial Area Association** ([dia-association.com](https://dia-association.com/directories/), which has a member directory), Hoskote/KIADB industrial area associations, CILT India (Bengaluru chapter), Warehousing Association of India, India Warehousing Show, LogiMAT India.
- **KIADB industrial areas:** Hoskote, Doddaballapur, Dabaspete, Nelamangala, Narasapura (Kolar), Aerospace Park (Devanahalli), Harohalli. Walk them; every factory gate is a prospect for outsourced warehousing.

## ICP 3 — Referral Partners

| Partner | Who exactly | What they want | What we give |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Industrial brokers** (IPC industrial desks + local) | Industrial & Logistics leasing teams; independent warehouse brokers | Commission; *inventory to show tenants* (vacancy is high, but good BTS options are scarce) | Co-broking on BTS; exclusive "pipeline" land they can pitch to tenants; leasing commission splits |
| **PEB companies** | BD/sales heads of national + Bangalore PEB firms | Steel tonnage orders | Two-way: we send them projects (in our tender matrix); they send us landowners who ask "who'll get me a tenant/loan?" |
| **Bank/NBFC LRD teams** | Relationship managers in commercial real-estate / LRD / construction finance | Good-quality loan files with strong tenants | Bankable projects, clean documentation; we make their file easy |
| **CAs** | CAs serving landowning families and SME owners in Hoskote, KR Puram, Whitefield, Nelamangala, Peenya, Yeshwanthpur, Doddaballapur | Look good to clients; retain clients | Co-hosted "Land income and tax" seminars; tax-structure inputs. **No cash referral fees to practising CAs** (see file 08) |
| **Wealth advisors / private bankers** | MFDs, private-bank RMs, family offices | Grow client AUM; "real asset" ideas | Content, client events, quick feasibility for their clients |
| **Lawyers / surveyors / document writers** | Title lawyers, licensed surveyors near the SROs | Fees, clients | Referral fees (non-regulated partners); we send them title and survey work |
| **Land aggregators / mid-size brokers** | Brokers who sell land on the corridors | Commission | A fee for introducing owners who *don't* want to sell (so they earn instead of losing the deal) |
