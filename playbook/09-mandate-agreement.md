# 09 — Development Mandate: Key Terms and Fee Schedule

> **Note for Yash:** the mandate protects three things: **our fee** (so a landowner can't take our feasibility work to a cheaper contractor), **the client** (so they know exactly what they pay and get), and **our reputation** (the open-book rule in writing). Keep the language plain; a Kannada summary is mandatory.

---

## 1. Recommended fee schedule (resolves the open decision)

### 1A. Development management fee (the transparent-fee model)

| Construction cost (excl. land, excl. GST) | Fee | Minimum fee |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹15 Cr | **4.0%** | ₹45 L |
| ₹15–30 Cr | **3.5%** | — |
| Above ₹30 Cr | **3.0%** | — |

- Charged on the **actual final construction cost** (vendor invoices), with a **cap at the approved budget + 5%**, so we earn nothing extra from overruns. *(This one clause makes our incentive obvious to the client: we want the project on budget.)*
- **Payment milestones:**

| Milestone | % of fee |
|---|---|
| Mandate signing (retainer; feasibility fee credited here) | 10% |
| Approvals received (conversion + plan approval) | 15% |
| Tender awarded / contracts signed | 10% |
| Construction progress (monthly, pro-rata to certified progress) | 45% |
| Handover + occupancy/completion certificate | 15% |
| Rent commencement (tenant pays first rent) | 5% |

### 1B. Other fees

| Service | Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Land Potential Snapshot | Free | Lead magnet |
| Detailed Feasibility Report | ₹1.5 L (≤5 acres) · ₹2.25 L (5–10) · ₹3 L (10+) | 50% upfront, 50% on delivery; **100% credited** to the development fee if the mandate is signed within 90 days |
| **Leasing commission** | **1.5 months' rent** (we source the tenant directly) · **1 month** (co-broked: the outside broker takes the tenant-side fee) | Payable on lease registration. 50% on LOI-to-lease conversion is acceptable if the client wants staging |
| **LRD / construction-finance arrangement** | **0.75% of sanctioned amount** (min ₹5 L) | Payable on first disbursement. If a lender also pays a DSA/arranger fee, we **disclose it and credit it** to the client, as the open-book rule requires |
| **Asset management** (optional, from rent start) | **3% of rent collected** | Rent collection, escalation tracking, renewals, tenant relations, compliance calendar, annual report |
| **Facility management** | Annual contract; typically ₹1.0–1.7/sq ft/month (≈ ₹20–30 L/yr on 1.5 L sq ft), often recovered from the tenant as CAM | Security, housekeeping, MEP maintenance, fire-system AMC |
| **Fit-out** (racking, forklifts, docks) | Quoted separately by the sister company | **Must be disclosed as a related party**, with at least one competing quote offered |
| **Design-and-build (from Year 2)** | Fixed price / GMP with **8–12% margin built in**, disclosed as a lump sum | **Never combined** with the development fee on the same project. The client chooses Model A *or* Model B |

### 1C. Worked example (1.5 L sq ft, ₹27 Cr construction, rent ₹21/sq ft)
- Development fee 3.5% → **₹94.5 L**
- Leasing commission 1.5 months × ₹31.5 L/month → **₹47 L** (₹31.5 L if co-broked)
- LRD arrangement 0.75% × ₹22 Cr → **₹16.5 L**
- **One-time total ≈ ₹1.4–1.6 Cr** · Recurring: asset management ~₹11 L/yr + FM ₹20–30 L/yr

> **Our two models, side by side, in every proposal:**
> **Model A — Managed Development:** you contract the vendors directly; we manage them for a disclosed fee.
> **Model B — Design & Build (from Year 2):** we contract everything at a fixed or guaranteed maximum price; our margin is inside the price, and we tell you the margin %.
> **Never both on one project.**

## 2. Key clauses (term sheet for the lawyer)

1. **Parties:** Sthira Developments Pvt Ltd and the Landowner(s). **All co-owners on the RTC sign**, or give a registered GPA to one representative.
2. **Scope:** the 7 stages (feasibility, approvals and structuring, design and budget, construction management, financing, leasing, handover/operate), with a list of deliverables per stage. Optional services (asset management, FM) by separate schedule.
3. **Exclusivity:** an exclusive mandate for **development management and leasing** of the identified land for **18 months** from signing, extendable by mutual consent. During exclusivity the landowner will not appoint another developer, PM, JDA partner or leasing agent for the land.
4. **Tail clause:** if, within **15 months** after termination or expiry, the landowner develops, leases or enters a JDA for the land **with any party introduced by us, or using our feasibility/design work product**, the fees for the relevant stages are payable as if we had done the work. (12–18 months is the market range; 15 is a fair middle.)
5. **Sale carve-out:** if the landowner decides to **sell** the land during the mandate, there is no development fee. If the buyer was introduced by us, a finder's fee of **1% of the sale value** applies. (Being fair to families who change their minds earns referrals.)
6. **Open-book covenant (our promise in writing):**
   - We earn **only** the fees in this schedule. No commissions, discounts, rebates or spreads from contractors, vendors or lenders. Any such payment offered to us is passed to the client.
   - Every tender shows **at least three bids** (where the market permits), with our recommendation; the client approves the award.
   - Related-party vendors (e.g., our sister equipment company) are **disclosed** and must compete on a quote.
   - The client has audit rights over the project books.
7. **Client contracts directly with vendors (Model A)**, on our standard contract templates containing: **5% retention** (released after the defects-liability period of 12 months), a **5–10% performance bank guarantee**, liquidated damages for delay (e.g., 0.5% per week, capped at 5–10%), and warranty clauses (roof 10 years, waterproofing, floor).
8. **Budget and timeline:** the approved budget and master schedule are annexed. Any change needs a written **change order** approved by the client. We report weekly (WhatsApp summary) and monthly (written report + site photos + cost-to-complete).
9. **Fee protection:** fees are payable within **15 days** of invoice; interest at 18% p.a. on late payment; we may suspend services after 30 days of non-payment.
10. **Client obligations:** provide title documents, sign applications, fund the project per the drawdown schedule, make decisions within 7 days, and not interfere with contractors directly (route through the project head).
11. **Leasing authority:** we are the exclusive leasing agent for the mandate period. We may co-broke with other brokers. Lease terms (rent floor, lock-in, tenant type) are agreed with the client in a **leasing brief** before marketing.
12. **Conflict of interest:** we disclose in writing if our group is also developing a competing site within 5 km and seeking tenants of the same type. Tenant introductions are offered to the client's project first where its specs and timeline fit the tenant. *(This matters because the family also originates its own Hoskote-style projects.)*
13. **Referral disclosure:** any referral fee we pay to the introducer is disclosed (amount or %) in the mandate.
14. **Limitation of liability:** our liability is capped at the fees received; no liability for contractor defaults beyond enforcing the contracts, guarantees and retentions; no guarantee of approvals, rent or tenant (we commit to *process and effort*, with clear KPIs).
15. **Termination:** either party may terminate on 60 days' notice. Fees earned to date are payable plus the tail clause. Immediate termination for material breach.
16. **Confidentiality, non-solicitation** of our staff for 12 months.
17. **Taxes:** GST at 18% on fees, extra. TDS as applicable.
18. **Disputes:** mediation → arbitration, Bengaluru, sole arbitrator.
19. **Language:** English agreement + **Kannada summary of key terms**, signed by all owners, plus a family meeting where it's read aloud and explained. Record that the meeting happened.

## 3. Proposal-to-signing process

1. Feasibility report presentation (in person, whole family present).
2. Send a **mandate summary** (2 pages, Kannada + English) within 48 hours.
3. Family consults their lawyer/CA; we offer a call with them.
4. Sign at our office or their home, with a small ceremony: sweets and a photo with consent. It's a big moment for a family.
5. Within 7 days: kickoff meeting, WhatsApp project group created, project head introduced, first weekly update sent.
