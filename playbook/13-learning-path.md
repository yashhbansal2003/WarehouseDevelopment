# 13 — Yash's Marketing Learning Path (learn it the month you use it)

> **The principle:** never learn a skill months before you need it. Each month below matches the calendar (file 10). Learn the module in **~4–5 hours a month**, use it the same week, and write a short lesson in your "Marketing Lab" log. In 12 months you'll have done what most marketers only read about, and you'll have the data to prove it.

| Month | Skill | Learn (free / cheap) | Do it this month | You've learned it when… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Oct** | **Positioning:** who we're for and why we're different | Book: *Obviously Awesome* (April Dunford), chapters 1–6 | Finalise brand + pillars (file 01); write your 1-sentence pitch; test it on 10 people | A stranger can repeat our promise back to you after hearing it once |
| **2 · Nov** | **Customer research:** asking questions that get the truth | Book: *The Mom Test* (Rob Fitzpatrick), a short read | 10 landowner + 5 broker conversations using the script questions; record exact phrases | You have 20 real customer quotes in a doc, and you've changed one script because of them |
| **3 · Dec** | **Copywriting:** headlines, offers, calls to action | *Building a StoryBrand* (Donald Miller); free: Copyhackers blog | Write the website copy + 15 Google headlines; A/B test 2 landing-page headlines | One headline beats another by a measurable margin |
| **4 · Jan** | **Google Ads** | **Google Skillshop** (free): *Google Ads Search Certification* | Run G1/G2 yourself for 2 weeks before handing to a freelancer; do the search-terms review every Monday | You can explain your CPC, CTR, conversion rate and cost per qualified lead without looking |
| **5 · Feb** | **Meta Ads** | **Meta Blueprint** (free): "Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate" learning path | Build M2 with 4 creatives; read the breakdown by age/placement; kill losers | You know which creative and audience produce the cheapest *qualified* lead, not the cheapest lead |
| **6 · Mar** | **Sales conversations & closing** | *SPIN Selling* (Neil Rackham); *Never Split the Difference* (Chris Voss) | Record (with consent) or debrief 5 feasibility presentations; practise the objection table | Feasibility → mandate conversion improves; you handle "let me think" without pressure |
| **7 · Apr** | **Analytics & funnels** | Google Analytics 4 course (Skillshop, free); HubSpot Academy *Inbound Marketing* (free) | Build the monthly funnel dashboard; connect offline conversions | You can name the bottleneck in the funnel this month and the fix |
| **8 · May** | **Content & personal brand** | *Show Your Work!* (Austin Kleon); study 5 founders who post well on LinkedIn | Analyse your 30 posts: which hooks won? Write 4 posts using the winning pattern | Your average post reach doubles vs months 1–3 |
| **9 · Jun** | **B2B / account-based marketing** | LinkedIn Marketing Labs (free); *The Challenger Sale* | Run the 100-account occupier programme with LinkedIn Ads + outreach | 10 named occupier accounts are in active conversation |
| **10 · Jul** | **Persuasion & trust** | *Influence* (Robert Cialdini) | Audit every asset for social proof, authority, consistency; add testimonials and data | Site-visit → feasibility conversion improves |
| **11 · Aug** | **Offers & pricing** | *$100M Offers* (Alex Hormozi) | Design the Year-2 design-and-build offer and the asset-management upsell | You can present 2 offers so a client easily picks one |
| **12 · Sep** | **Marketing strategy & planning** | *This Is Marketing* (Seth Godin); re-read your Marketing Lab log | Write the Year-2 marketing plan yourself; Claude reviews it | Your plan is driven by your own data, not assumptions |

## Habits that make you excellent

1. **Marketing Lab log:** one entry per experiment (hypothesis → change → spend → result → decision).
2. **Swipe file:** screenshot every ad, post or brochure that makes *you* stop. Review monthly.
3. **Talk to 5 customers a week, even when busy.** Every great marketer is a great listener.
4. **Weekly 30-min "numbers hour"** (Monday dashboard). Marketing without numbers is decoration.
5. **Teach what you learn:** your LinkedIn posts about what you learned are content, and teaching locks in learning.
6. **Use Claude in this project as your sparring partner:** paste your weekly dashboard, draft posts, ad results or a tricky landowner objection, and ask for critique. Ask "what would you test next?" every month.
