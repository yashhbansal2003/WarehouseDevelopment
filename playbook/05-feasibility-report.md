# 05 — Feasibility Report Template

Two products: the free **Land Potential Snapshot** (2 pages, 72 hours), which is the lead magnet, and the paid **Detailed Feasibility Report** (15–20 pages, 2 weeks), which is the first commitment. The free one must be good enough to impress and short enough to make the paid one feel necessary.

---

## A. Land Potential Snapshot (FREE, 2 pages)

**Inputs needed:** survey no. + village, acres, Google Maps pin, one approach-road photo. **Desk sources:** Bhoomi RTC, Dishank map, the BMRDA/local-authority Master Plan zoning map, Google Earth, our rent database.

**Page 1 — "What your land can become"**
- Header: owner name, survey no., village, taluk, acres, map thumbnail with the nearest highway marked
- Three big numbers:
  - **Buildable warehouse area:** ~X sq ft (at a ~50–55% ground coverage assumption)
  - **Indicative build cost:** ₹A–B Cr (three tiers: basic / Grade-A / high-spec)
  - **Indicative rent:** ₹C–D L per month (at ₹20–24/sq ft; we always show a range)
- Traffic-light checks (🟢🟡🔴): road width · distance to highway · zoning · title (visible from RTC) · land shape · flood/low-lying
- The 3 biggest risks or open items, in plain words

**Page 2 — "Your options and next step"**
- A table of Sell / JDA / **Develop & keep** with an indicative 20-year income for each (very rough, clearly labelled *indicative*)
- What the detailed report adds (title review, approvals path, real vendor quotes, bank-ready cash flow, tenant shortlist)
- A photo of Hoskote 001 + the visit invite (QR code to book a Saturday slot)
- Disclaimer: *"Indicative estimate based on public data. Not an offer or a guarantee of rent, cost or approvals."*

**Format:** English + Kannada versions, generated from one Excel/Google Sheet template, so it takes about 90 minutes per report once you've done 10.

## B. Detailed Feasibility Report (PAID, ₹1.5–3 L, 15–20 pages)

| Page | Section | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cover** | Project name ("[Village] Logistics Park – Feasibility"), owner, date, Sthira + "A Bansal Group company", confidentiality line |
| 2 | **Executive summary (1 page that decides everything)** | Recommendation (Go / Go with conditions / No-go); best development option; total project cost; expected rent; net monthly income to owner; payback; timeline from today to rent start; top 3 risks + mitigations |
| 3 | **Site profile** | Location map (highway, airport, city distances, nearby warehouses and occupiers); access road width; topography; photos; utilities (power line, HT availability, water, drainage) |
| 4 | **Title and legal status** (from our panel lawyer) | RTC/mutation history, ownership chain (30 years where available), encumbrance certificate (Kaveri), co-owners, litigation search, red flags. Opinion: clear / curable / not clear |
| 5 | **Zoning and approvals path** | Master-Plan zoning; land conversion status (Sec. 95 KLR Act) and route; plan approval authority; fire NOC; KSPCB consent; BESCOM HT; environmental thresholds; **approvals timeline Gantt** + government fees estimate |
| 6 | **Market analysis** | Corridor demand (C&W/JLL/Savills numbers); recent comparable leases (e.g., DHL Hoskote ₹23.50, Amazon Nelamangala ₹26.25) and asking rents within 10 km; vacancy and new supply within 10 km; who's leasing on this corridor |
| 7 | **Target tenant profile** | The 2–3 tenant types this site suits best and why; named examples of companies active nearby; spec they'll expect |
| 8 | **Concept layout** | Site plan: building footprint, setbacks, truck circulation, dock count, parking, ground coverage/FAR used, phase 1/phase 2 option |
| 9 | **Building specification, three tiers** | Basic / Grade-A / High-spec: clear height, floor (Trimix vs FM2 VDF, load tonnes/m²), roof (single skin vs insulated), docks, fire (hydrant only vs sprinklers), lighting, solar-ready, certification (IGBC/LEED optional) |
| 10 | **Cost estimate, three tiers** | Line-item cost per tier (land development, civil, PEB, roofing, flooring, fire, electrical/HT, plumbing/STP, docks, roads, compound wall, approvals, consultants, our fee, contingency 5–7%) + ₹/sq ft. **Sourced from the vendor matrix with named quotes** |
| 11 | **Development options compared** | (a) Owner self-funds + loan (fee model); (b) revenue-share lease like Hoskote 001; (c) JDA; (d) outright sale. 20-year owner income and risk for each |
| 12 | **Financing plan** | Equity vs debt; construction-loan terms (indicative from 2–3 lenders); LRD take-out after leasing (LTV, rate, tenor); drawdown schedule |
| 13 | **Cash flow & returns** | 20-year cash flow: rent, 5%/3-yr or annual escalation as applicable, rent-free period (assume 2–3 months), vacancy allowance, FM, insurance, property tax, debt service. Outputs: yield on cost, owner IRR, DSCR, payback year |
| 14 | **Sensitivity** | Table: rent ±10%, cost ±10%, 6-month delay, 6-month vacancy at lease end. **Show the downside honestly** |
| 15 | **Timeline** | Month-by-month: feasibility → approvals → tender → construction → handover → rent start. Typical: **10–14 months** to rent start where conversion is pending; **7–9 months** where conversion is done |
| 16 | **Risk register** | Top 10 risks: title, approvals, cost escalation (steel), contractor default, tenant default, vacancy, interest rates, monsoon, access road, co-owner disputes. Mitigation + owner of each |
| 17 | **Our proposal** | 7-stage scope; fee schedule (from file 09); the feasibility fee credit; team; reporting cadence (weekly WhatsApp update + monthly report) |
| 18 | **Next steps** | Three actions with dates (e.g., "sign mandate", "share title documents", "family meeting") |
| Annex | **Assumptions & sources** | Every number with its source and date. Disclaimers |

**Quality standard:** a bank credit officer should be able to read pages 2, 10, 12 and 13 and understand the loan. A 65-year-old landowner should be able to read page 2 (in Kannada) and understand the decision.

**Production:** QS/estimator builds pages 9–14 from the master model; the lawyer delivers page 4; the approvals consultant does page 5; the leasing head does pages 6–7; you write pages 2 and 17. Turnaround: 10 working days.
