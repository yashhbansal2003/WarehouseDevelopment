# 15 — The Lead Engine: Finding Real, Genuine Outside Leads

> **The one idea behind this file:** the right leads are people who are **already showing a signal** that they want to do something with their land, or need warehouse space. Don't shout at everyone. **Find the signals, get there first, and filter hard.**
>
> Friends and family are excluded here. Everything below reaches strangers.

---

## 1. What is a "right lead"? (score every lead the same day)

A lead is only worth your time if it passes this filter. Score each lead in the CRM:

### Landowner lead score (out of 10)
| Check | Points |
|---|---|
| Land on our belts (Hoskote/NH-75, Nelamangala–Dabaspete/NH-48, Doddaballapur/STRR, Devanahalli) within ~5 km of a highway | 3 |
| 3–15 acres in one block | 2 |
| Approach road ≥ 9–12 m (or can be widened) | 1 |
| Title in the family's name, ≤4 co-owners, no litigation known | 2 |
| Talking to the decision-maker (or they're in the room) | 1 |
| Wants income within ~12 months (not "just exploring") | 1 |

**A-lead (8–10):** call or visit within 24 hours, offer the Snapshot plus a site visit.
**B-lead (5–7):** Snapshot within 72 hours, then follow up monthly.
**C-lead (0–4):** polite reply, add to the newsletter, no time spent.

### Occupier lead score
| Check | Points |
|---|---|
| Requirement ≥ 40,000 sq ft | 3 |
| Location fits our belts | 2 |
| Timeline ≤ 12 months | 2 |
| Decision-maker or direct influencer | 2 |
| Specific spec need (height, floor, docks, size) that ready stock can't meet | 1 |

> **Measure every source by "A-leads per month" and "cost per A-lead"**, never by total leads. 100 junk leads is worse than 5 A-leads, because junk eats your time.

## 2. Landowner leads: 8 sources ranked by quality

### Source 1: "Wanted" ads (land owners come to you) ⭐ Start this week
Instead of chasing owners, **advertise that you want land**. Owners thinking about their land respond on their own, which is the strongest signal there is.

**Where to post:**
| Platform | How | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com** | Post a "Requirement" / "Wanted" listing: *"Wanted: 3–15 acres on long lease for warehouse — Hoskote / Nelamangala / Doddaballapur. Owner keeps the land. Monthly rent."* | Free–₹5k/month (paid plans boost visibility) |
| **1acre.in** (Karnataka land marketplace) | List the requirement; also browse their land listings daily (see Source 2) | Free/low |
| **OLX + Facebook Marketplace** (Land category, Bangalore Rural) | Same ad, in Kannada and English | Free |
| **Facebook groups**: search "Hoskote real estate", "Nelamangala land", "Devanahalli plots", "Bangalore land owners", "Doddaballapur property" | Join 20–30 groups; post the wanted ad weekly (Kannada + English); reply to land posts | Free |
| **Kannada newspapers**: *Prajavani*, *Vijay Karnataka*, *Udayavani* classifieds, Bengaluru Rural editions | Classified: *"ಗೋದಾಮು ನಿರ್ಮಾಣಕ್ಕಾಗಿ 3–15 ಎಕರೆ ಜಮೀನು ದೀರ್ಘಾವಧಿ ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆಗೆ ಬೇಕಾಗಿದೆ. ಜಮೀನು ನಿಮ್ಮದೇ. ಪ್ರತಿ ತಿಂಗಳು ಬಾಡಿಗೆ."* + WhatsApp number | ₹2–8k per insertion |
| **Flex boards** at 10 spots: NH-75 Hoskote toll area, Hoskote town circles, Nelamangala junction, Dabaspete, Doddaballapur bus stand, Sub-Registrar office roads, APMC yards | "Own 3+ acres near the highway? Earn monthly rent without selling. WhatsApp: ___" | ₹3–6k per board per month |

**Why it works:** most land ads in these areas are *selling* ads. A *wanted-on-lease* ad is unusual, so it stands out, and it attracts exactly the owner who doesn't want to sell, who is our ICP.

### Source 2: Land "for sale" and "for JD/lease" listings (intent signal) ⭐ Start this week
Every owner listing land on a portal has **already decided to do something with it**. Many would prefer income over a sale if someone showed them how.

**Daily routine (30 min):** search 99acres, MagicBricks, 1acre.in, NoBroker, OLX, Housing and Facebook groups for:
- "agricultural land Hoskote", "industrial land Hoskote", "land for joint development Nelamangala", "land for lease Doddaballapur", "converted land Dabaspete", "land near STRR"
- Filter: 3+ acres. Save every listing to the CRM (link, size, location, contact, owner or broker).

**Message to the lister (WhatsApp):**
> "Namaskara, I saw your listing for ___ acres in ___. We're Sthira Developments (Bansal Group). Before you sell, would you like to see what the land could earn as **monthly rent** if a warehouse were built on it? We'll make a free 2-page report in 3 days. No obligation. Many owners choose to keep their land and earn instead."

If it's a broker listing, use the broker script and offer the referral fee: the broker earns even if the owner doesn't sell.

### Source 3: Data-led field prospecting (the systematic engine)
You don't wait for owners. You **map every suitable parcel** and reach each owner.
1. **Dishank app**: mark every undeveloped parcel ≥3 acres within 5 km of the highways. Target: **150 parcels** in 3 months.
2. **Bhoomi RTC** (landrecords.karnataka.gov.in): owner names and extent for each survey number.
3. **Prioritise by signal:** a parcel next to a new warehouse, a conversion board on the land, a "for sale" board, a new road being laid, or recent sales nearby (check Kaveri 2.0 for registrations).
4. **Reach the owner** through the local network: the village document writer, the panchayat member, the neighbouring farmer, or the broker who knows them. Go *warm*, never with a cold knock carrying a printout.
5. **Target: field BD reaches 20 new owners/week.**

### Source 4: Land intermediaries (paid referral network)
These people see land owners every week. Each one can bring a steady trickle of leads.
| Who | Where | Pitch |
|---|---|---|
| **Document writers / stamp vendors** | Outside the Hoskote, Nelamangala, Doddaballapur and Devanahalli Sub-Registrar offices | ₹25k on signing + 10% of fee. Give them a stack of Kannada Partner Cards |
| **Licensed surveyors** | Taluk offices | Same; also send them our survey work |
| **Land-title advocates** | Near the taluk courts and SROs | Same (advocates in practice: check Bar Council rules; offer work referrals instead of cash where needed) |
| **Local land brokers** (from portal listings + field) | Every village/town | "Owner won't sell? Bring them to us. You still earn." |
| **Village accountants' circle / ex-panchayat members** | Villages | Respectful relationship; introductions, not lists |
| **Transport / quarry / petrol-pump owners** on the highways | Highway | Often landowners themselves, or know them all |

**Target:** 40 active intermediaries by month 3, each giving 1 lead every 1–2 months, which is 20–40 leads a month.

### Source 5: Google Search ads (people actively searching)
Low volume but **high intent**. Someone typing "lease my land for warehouse" is ready to act.
- Keywords and negatives are in file 07 §2B. Bid only on **owner-intent** phrases: "lease land for warehouse", "build warehouse on my land", "land for joint development bangalore", "warehouse rent per sq ft hoskote", "warehouse construction cost per sq ft".
- **Kannada + English** landing pages; short form; WhatsApp button.
- **Budget:** ₹1,000–2,000/day. **Expected:** roughly 1–3 genuine owner leads a week at first (it's a niche search; that's fine, because these are high quality).

### Source 6: Meta ads (Facebook/Instagram) to owners *and their children*
Higher volume and more junk, so the **filter questions in the lead form are essential** (file 07 §3B).
- **Audience 1, owners:** 35–65, within 15 km radius pins around Hoskote, Nelamangala, Dabaspete, Doddaballapur, Devanahalli. Kannada video creative.
- **Audience 2, the IT son/daughter:** 26–45, Bangalore city, English creative: *"Your family's land near Hoskote is earning nothing. Here's what it could earn."*
- **Use click-to-WhatsApp** (owners prefer chatting to forms) and Instant Forms with "higher intent" turned on. Test both.
- **Budget:** ₹1,000–1,500/day. **Expected:** 15–40 leads/week, of which maybe 10–25% are A/B.

### Source 7: YouTube (Kannada) + local Kannada influencers
- Run the Hoskote series as **YouTube in-feed ads** targeted to Bangalore Rural/North districts.
- **Partner with 2–3 Kannada YouTube channels** covering real estate, farming or land law (search "land conversion Kannada", "agriculture land Karnataka" on YouTube; pick channels with 20k–300k subscribers and comments from local farmers). Pay ₹10–40k for an explainer episode featuring our model. One good video brings leads for months.

### Source 8: Landowner meetings (one-to-many)
Once Sources 3–4 give you a foothold in a village, convert it into a meeting for 15–30 owners (file 04 §7). Promote with flex boards, a WhatsApp voice note from the local convenor, a Kannada newspaper insert, and Meta radius ads (5 km around the venue). **One meeting typically yields 5–10 Snapshot requests.**

## 4. Lead-handling rules (where most companies lose genuine leads)

1. **Reply within 15 minutes** (8 am–8 pm). Set the WhatsApp auto-reply, and assign one person on duty each day.
2. **Call, don't just text.** Every A/B lead gets a phone call within 24 hours.
3. **Qualify on the first call** using the score in §1, and write the score in the CRM.
4. **Give value fast:** Snapshot in 72 hours. Speed signals professionalism.
5. **Follow up 6 times** over 6 weeks before giving up (file 04 §1D). Most deals close after the 4th contact.
6. **Every lead gets a source tag** (e.g., "99acres-wanted", "FB-group-Hoskote", "Meta-KN-video", "Doc-writer-Ravi"), so we know what works.
7. **Brokers who respond to owner ads** are welcomed into the partner programme. They're not junk.
