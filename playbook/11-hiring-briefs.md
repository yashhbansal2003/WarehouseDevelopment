# 11 — Hiring Briefs

Two hires decide whether the ground engine works: the **Field BD (Kannada, local land networks)** and the **Leasing / BD Head**. Hire both by month 2–4.

## A. Field Business Development Executive: Landowner Relations (Kannada)

### Job post (copy-paste for LinkedIn / Naukri / WhatsApp groups / local newspaper)

> **Field BD Executive: Landowner Relations (Hoskote / Nelamangala / Doddaballapur)**
> **Sthira Developments (A Bansal Group company)** — Bengaluru
>
> We help landowning families earn monthly rent by developing warehouses on their land, without selling it. We're looking for someone who **knows the villages and land networks** around Hoskote, Nelamangala–Dabaspete or Doddaballapur, speaks **fluent Kannada**, and enjoys meeting people every day.
>
> **You will:**
> - Meet 15–20 landowners, land brokers, village leaders and document writers every week
> - Map land parcels (survey numbers, owners, road access) using the Dishank app and Bhoomi records
> - Organise landowner meetings (15–30 people) and Saturday site visits to our Hoskote warehouse
> - Build and look after our network of local referral partners
> - Keep every contact updated in our CRM (we'll train you)
> - Appear in our Kannada videos explaining warehouse development (we'll train you)
>
> **You have:**
> - Fluent Kannada (spoken); working English; Hindi is a plus
> - Roots or a strong network in at least one of these belts
> - 2–8 years in land/real-estate sales, rural banking, agri-inputs, insurance, cooperative societies, or as a land broker/aide
> - Own two-wheeler + licence; comfortable with WhatsApp, Google Maps, smartphone apps
> - Honest. People in your area trust you
>
> **We offer:** ₹35,000–55,000/month fixed + travel allowance + **incentives**: ₹5,000 per qualified Snapshot lead, ₹10,000 per site visit that buys a feasibility report, **₹1,00,000 per signed mandate**. Learning, a clear growth path to Partner Network Manager, and a chance to build something your community will see.
>
> Apply: WhatsApp your name + area + 3 lines about yourself to [number].

### Where to find this person
- Referrals from the **document writers, surveyors and land brokers** you already meet. Ask: "Who is the most trusted young person in this area who knows land?"
- Rural bank / cooperative society / microfinance field staff (Karnataka Gramin Bank, SKDRDP, Ujjivan, Spandana field officers).
- Real-estate plot-sales executives at layout developers on the Hoskote/Devanahalli belts.
- Local Kannada WhatsApp/Facebook job groups; Naukri; Apna app (excellent for field roles).

### Interview: 4 tests
1. **Network test:** "Name 10 landowners or land brokers you could call this week, and what you know about each." (Pass: ≥6 credible names.)
2. **Role-play:** play a suspicious 65-year-old landowner. Ask them to explain our model in Kannada in 2 minutes.
3. **Field test (paid half-day):** give 3 survey numbers; they find the owners' names, road width and nearby landmarks, and report back by WhatsApp.
4. **Reference check:** speak to 2 people in their village/area (not just former employers).

### 90-day scorecard
| By day 30 | By day 60 | By day 90 |
|---|---|---|
| 40 parcels mapped · 20 landowner conversations · 10 partners met | First landowner meeting organised · 6 Snapshots from their leads · 3 site visits | 100 parcels mapped · 15 Snapshots · 1 paid feasibility from their leads |

## B. Head of Leasing & Business Development

### Job post

> **Head: Leasing & Business Development**
> **Sthira Developments (A Bansal Group company)** — Bengaluru
>
> We're building an open-book warehouse development company for 50,000–3,00,000 sq ft built-to-suit projects on Bengaluru's Hoskote, Nelamangala–Dabaspete and Doddaballapur belts. Our flagship 1,13,000 sq ft Hoskote project is under construction.
>
> **You will:**
> - Own leasing for Hoskote 001 and every client project: tenant sourcing, proposals, LOIs, lease negotiation, registration
> - Build relationships with the real-estate heads of 3PLs, e-commerce, manufacturers and FMCG companies, and with the industrial desks of CBRE, JLL, C&W, Colliers, Knight Frank and Savills
> - Shape building specs with tenants and our project team
> - Help structure LRD with lenders (tenant credit, lease terms)
> - Lead corporate BD: the 100-account occupier programme, LinkedIn presence, events
> - Grow into a partner/leadership role as the company scales
>
> **You have:**
> - 7–15 years in **industrial & logistics leasing** (an IPC industrial desk, a warehousing developer such as ESR / IndoSpace / Welspun One / Horizon / Greenbase, or a 3PL's real-estate team)
> - A personal network across Bangalore 3PL and e-commerce real-estate decision-makers
> - At least 1 msf of closed industrial leases (list them)
> - Strong lease-document skills (lock-in, escalation, CAM, fit-out, security deposit, rent-free periods)
> - English + Kannada or Hindi
>
> **We offer:** ₹28–45 L fixed (depending on experience) + **leasing incentive: 10–15% of leasing commission collected** + an **ESOP / profit-share** from Year 2. Entrepreneurial, with a founder who backs you.

### Where to find this person
- LinkedIn search: "Industrial & Logistics" + Bengaluru at CBRE/JLL/C&W/Colliers/Knight Frank/Savills; "Leasing" at ESR, IndoSpace, Welspun One, Horizon, LOGOS, Greenbase, KSH; "Real Estate" at Delhivery, Mahindra Logistics, TVS SCS, DHL SC, Flipkart, Amazon.
- Mid-level people (Associate Director/AVP) at IPCs who want ownership and upside are the sweet spot.
- Use a niche real-estate recruiter only if direct search fails in 6 weeks (fee is typically 8.33% of annual CTC).

### Interview tests
1. **Deal walkthrough:** "Walk me through your best and your worst industrial lease, with numbers."
2. **Network proof:** "Who are the 10 people you'd call this week about Hoskote 001?" (They should name actual roles at real companies.)
3. **Case exercise (take-home, 48 hrs):** "Here's Hoskote 001. Write the leasing brief: target tenants, asking rent, concessions, timeline, risks." Judge the realism (rent ₹21–24, rent-free months, lock-in).
4. **Reference calls:** 2 former bosses + 2 clients/brokers.

### 90-day scorecard
| By day 30 | By day 60 | By day 90 |
|---|---|---|
| Hoskote leasing brief + marketing deck · IPC desks briefed (all 6) | 30 occupier meetings · 3 Hoskote tenant site visits | Hoskote tenant shortlist of 2–3 + LOI in negotiation · 2 BTS requirements in play for client sites |
