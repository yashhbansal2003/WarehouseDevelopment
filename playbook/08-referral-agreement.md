# 08 — Referral Partner Agreement: Key Terms

> **Note for Yash:** a referral programme only works if partners **trust that they'll be paid** and **find it easy**. Make it one page, pay fast, and pay *every* time, even on small amounts. Your reputation among brokers in Hoskote will spread in weeks, good or bad.
>
> **Change from the original plan:** "10% of our fee within 30 days of signing" means paying ~₹9.5 L the month a mandate is signed, while our fee arrives over 12–18 months. That is a cash-flow trap. Below, a small **signing bonus** keeps the "fast payment" promise, and the main fee is paid **as we get paid**.

---

## 1. Partner tiers and fees

| Partner type | What they do | Fee | When paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Landowner introducer** (local broker, surveyor, document writer, village contact, friend) | Introduces a landowner who signs a **Development Mandate** | **10% of our development fee received** (≈ ₹4–10 L per project), **capped at ₹12 L per project** | ₹25,000 signing bonus within **15 days** of mandate signing; the balance pro-rata within **30 days** of each fee instalment we receive |
| Same, for a paid feasibility only | Landowner buys a Feasibility Report but doesn't sign (yet) | ₹10,000 flat | Within 15 days of report payment |
| **Industrial broker: tenant side** (co-broking on BTS) | Brings the occupier for our landowner's BTS project | Standard: they collect their tenant-side fee from the tenant; or, if the tenant won't pay, we share **50% of our leasing commission** | Within 30 days of us receiving the commission |
| **Industrial broker: landowner side** | Introduces a landowner | As "Landowner introducer" above | As above |
| **PEB company** | Introduces a landowner who signs | **Two-way, no cash:** guaranteed invitation to tender on that project + priority on 1 future tender. Optional ₹1 L thank-you if they prefer cash and their policy allows | — |
| **Loan syndicator / DSA** | Introduces a landowner or arranges a lender | 10% of our fee (as introducer) **or** their normal DSA payout from the lender, never both | As above |
| **CAs in practice / SEBI RIAs / bank employees** | Introduce clients | **No cash referral fee.** Professional and regulatory rules restrict practising CAs (ICAI Code of Ethics) and SEBI-registered investment advisers from taking referral commissions, and bank staff from personal payments. Give value instead: co-hosted client seminars, co-branded reports, priority service for their clients, public credit (with consent) | — |
| **Institutional sale introduction** (land too big for us, >25 acres) | We pass the lead to an institutional buyer/broker | We share 50% of any finder's fee we receive with the original introducer | On receipt |

> Have your CA confirm the TDS position on referral payments (generally Sec. 194H for commission, or 194J for professional fees) and GST (an introducer registered for GST invoices us with GST). Collect PAN + bank details at sign-up.

## 2. Key clauses (term sheet for the lawyer)

1. **Parties:** Sthira Developments Pvt Ltd and the Partner (individual or firm).
2. **Non-exclusive:** partners may work with others; we may work with other partners.
3. **Registration of an introduction ("protected lead"):** the Partner registers a landowner by WhatsApp/email with *owner name + survey no. + village*. We confirm within **3 working days** whether it's **new** (protected) or **already in our CRM** (not protected; we show the date we first logged it). First registered introducer wins.
4. **Protection period:** **18 months** from registration. If the landowner signs a mandate (or any development/lease deal with us or our group companies) within 18 months, the fee is payable.
5. **Fee basis:** calculated on **fees actually received** by us (excluding GST and reimbursements). If the client defaults or the mandate ends early, the referral fee reduces pro-rata. Fees already paid are never clawed back, except for fraud.
6. **Payment timing:** as in the table above. If we miss a payment date, we pay **1.5% per month interest**. (This clause signals seriousness. Partners love it.)
7. **Transparency to the client:** the Partner's fee is **disclosed to the landowner in the mandate**. This is consistent with our open-book promise. No secret commissions.
8. **Conduct:** the Partner must not represent themselves as our employee, make promises about rent, cost or approvals, or collect any money from the landowner on our behalf. Breach = termination + forfeiture of unpaid fees.
9. **Confidentiality:** landowner details and our feasibility work stay confidential.
10. **Anti-bribery:** no payments to government officials, bank staff or tenant employees. Immediate termination on breach.
11. **Term:** 2 years, auto-renew; either side can end it with 30 days' notice. Protected leads survive termination for their 18-month window.
12. **Taxes:** TDS deducted as applicable; Partner provides PAN; GST if registered.
13. **Disputes:** good-faith discussion → mediation → arbitration in Bengaluru (sole arbitrator), English/Kannada.
14. **Language:** a one-page Kannada summary attached. Local partners must understand what they sign.

## 3. Making the programme visible and easy

- **One-page "Partner Card"** (English + Kannada): who we want, what we pay, when, how to register. Hand it out at every SRO, broker office and village meeting.
- **WhatsApp registration:** "Send *REGISTER* + owner name + survey no. + village to [number]." The auto-reply confirms receipt.
- **Monthly partner statement:** every active partner gets a WhatsApp/email: their registered leads, status, fees earned, fees paid.
- **Recognition:** a quarterly "Partner Lunch" in Hoskote / Nelamangala; publicly thank top partners (with consent).
- **First-payment story:** when you pay your first referral fee, take a photo (with consent) and tell the story in broker WhatsApp groups. It's the best recruitment ad you'll ever run.
