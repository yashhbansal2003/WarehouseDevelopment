# 10 — 12-Month Calendar, Targets and KPIs (Oct 2026 – Sep 2027)

> **Note for Yash:** the first 90 days are about **building the machine** (brand, website, CRM, partners, content). Expect few mandates. Months 4–6 are about **turning the machine on** (ads at scale, meetings, site visits). Months 7–12 are about **making it efficient** (cut what doesn't work, double what does). Don't panic at month 3 with zero mandates. Do panic at month 3 if you have zero *conversations*.

## Month-by-month

### Month 1 — October 2026: **Foundations**
- **Brand:** finish the name checks (file 01), register the company/TM, buy domains, set up Google Workspace. Hire a brand designer (logo in 2 weeks).
- **Hire:** start the search for the field BD and the leasing/BD head (file 11). Brief the approvals consultant.
- **CRM:** set up Zoho CRM with the 3 pipelines; WhatsApp Business number; Interakt/WATI trial.
- **Hoskote 001:** rebuild the financial model with signed numbers (file 00 warning). Start the video series (Ep 1–3 shot).
- **Lists:** build the local broker list from portals (file 03 §A3); start the parcel map in Dishank (target 30 parcels).
- **LinkedIn:** optimise your profile (headline: "Building Bangalore's open-book warehouse developer | From your land to a leased warehouse"); post weeks 1–4.
- **Learning:** Module 1 (file 13).
- **KPIs:** 30 parcels mapped · 20 broker calls · 10 partner meetings · website brief signed · brand done.

### Month 2 — November 2026: **Build the machine**
- Website live (English, then Kannada by month end) with tracking (GA4, Ads tag, Pixel, Clarity).
- Brochure + Partner Card (English + Kannada) printed. Site hoarding at Hoskote with QR.
- Google Business Profile live.
- Snapshot template built (Excel + PDF design); do 5 practice Snapshots on known parcels.
- **Ads (test budget):** G1 Landowner Search + G3 Brand; M1 video views (to build the audience). Small daily budgets; learn.
- 15 partner meetings (brokers, 3 PEB companies, 3 lenders). Sign the first 5 referral agreements.
- **Field BD joins** (target). Parcel map → 60.
- **KPIs:** 40 conversations (all types) · 10 landowner conversations · 5 Snapshots · 5 referral agreements · 1st paid lead.

### Month 3 — December 2026: **First meetings, first visits**
- **First landowner meeting** (Hoskote belt, 15–30 people).
- Saturday site visits start (2nd and 4th Saturdays).
- Ads: add M2 (landowner leads) in Kannada + English; Experiment #2 (Kannada vs English).
- Occupier outreach begins: 25 of the 100 companies contacted (LinkedIn + email).
- Monthly "Bangalore Warehouse Monitor" #1 sent.
- **Q1 review** (last week): what worked, cost per lead, partner ranking, kill/keep decisions. Publish the honest LinkedIn post (week 12).
- **KPIs:** 20 landowner conversations · 8 Snapshots · 3 site visits · 1 paid feasibility · 10 referral agreements · 60 parcels mapped.

### Month 4 — January 2027: **Turn the engine on**
- **Leasing/BD head joins** (target). Hand over the occupier pipeline and the Hoskote leasing brief.
- Second landowner meeting (Nelamangala belt).
- Ads at full planned budget; set up offline conversion import (Qualified / Site visit / Mandate) to Google & Meta.
- LinkedIn Ads: Document Ads to the 100-account list.
- 50 of the 100 occupiers contacted.
- **KPIs:** 30 landowner conversations · 10 Snapshots · 4 site visits · 2 paid feasibility · **1st mandate (stretch)** · 15 partners signed.

### Month 5 — February 2027: **First mandate**
- **Target: Mandate #1 signed.** Celebrate publicly (with client consent): LinkedIn, WhatsApp, partner groups. Pay the first referral signing bonus fast and tell that story.
- Doddaballapur belt: first landowner meeting.
- First CA co-hosted seminar.
- Hoskote 001: tenant marketing in full swing (IPC brokers, direct).
- **KPIs:** 30 landowner conversations · 10 Snapshots · 4 site visits · 2 feasibility · 1 mandate · 75 occupiers contacted.

### Month 6 — March 2027: **Half-year review**
- **H1 review** against this calendar; reset targets; reallocate budget towards the 2 best channels.
- Media: pitch the Hoskote 001 story to ET Realty / a Kannada daily (if a milestone has been reached, e.g., roof complete or tenant LOI).
- All 100 occupiers contacted at least once; 10 in active conversation.
- Join BCIC or FKCCI; apply to speak at one event.
- **KPIs:** cumulative 2 mandates (stretch) · 6 feasibility · 25 site visits · cost per site visit known · partner leads ≥ 30% of the total.

### Month 7 — April 2027: **Systematise**
- Write the playbooks for the team: Snapshot SOP, site-visit SOP, partner onboarding SOP. (Anything done 10 times becomes an SOP.)
- Hoskote: target a **tenant LOI**.
- Third landowner meeting (Hoskote, a second cluster of villages).
- NRI landowner campaign test (Google + Meta, USA/UAE/UK).
- **KPIs:** 30 conversations · 10 Snapshots · 4 visits · 2 feasibility · 1 mandate in negotiation.

### Month 8 — May 2027: **Mandate #2**
- **Target: Mandate #2 signed.**
- First tender issued for Mandate #1 (vendor matrix in action: film it for content).
- Partner Lunch #1 (top 15 partners).
- **KPIs:** 2 mandates cumulative · 8–9 feasibility cumulative · 25 active partners.

### Month 9 — June 2027: **Occupier push**
- Occupier BTS: present the BTS site list to all IPC desks; target 1 occupier LOI on a client site.
- India Warehousing Show / LogiMAT (check dates): attend, with 30 pre-booked meetings.
- Monsoon content: drainage, waterproofing (Hoskote).
- **KPIs:** 1 BTS LOI (stretch) · 3 occupier site visits.

### Month 10 — July 2027: **Efficiency**
- Cut the bottom 30% of ad spend (by cost per site visit); scale the top channel by 30%.
- Fourth landowner meeting (Nelamangala/Dabaspete).
- Review referral economics: who produced mandates? Raise their tier.
- **KPIs:** cost per qualified lead down ≥ 25% vs month 4.

### Month 11 — August 2027: **Mandate #3 + Year-2 prep**
- **Target: Mandate #3.**
- Year-2 plan draft: design-and-build offer (Model B), team additions, budget.
- Hoskote 001 handover/rent-start content (if on schedule): the "first rent" episode.
- **KPIs:** 3 mandates cumulative · 12–15 feasibility cumulative.

### Month 12 — September 2027: **Year review**
- Publish the "Year 1 in numbers" case study (LinkedIn, website, PDF to all partners and leads).
- Hoskote 001 full case study published (if handover is done).
- Annual partner event. Team appraisals. Year-2 budget approved.
