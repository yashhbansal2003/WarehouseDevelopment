# 06 — Content Plan: LinkedIn (12 weeks) + the Hoskote 001 Video Series

> **Note for Yash: why content?** Ads *rent* attention; content *earns* trust. A landowner who has watched 5 Hoskote videos before meeting you is half-closed. A 3PL head who has seen 8 of your posts with real numbers takes your call. Content also makes ads cheaper: video viewers become your cheapest retargeting audience.
>
> **One shoot, many posts:** each week you shoot **one** Hoskote video. It becomes a YouTube video, an Instagram Reel, a WhatsApp status, a LinkedIn video and an ad creative. Don't create five things; create one thing and **cut it five ways**.

## 3. The Hoskote 001 video series: *"Land to Lease"*

**Series promise:** "Watch a 1,13,000 sq ft warehouse go from bare land to a leased building, with every cost, every contractor and every problem shown." Published weekly on YouTube (main), cut into Reels and Shorts. Kannada voice with English subtitles, or the other way round (test both).

**Production notes**
- **Kit (one-time ~₹1.5–2.5 L):** a good phone (or a mirrorless camera), DJI gimbal, 2 wireless lav mics (DJI Mic / Rode), DJI Mini drone (register on the DigitalSky platform and follow drone rules near the airport, since Hoskote is close to the KIA no-fly zone, so check the airspace map first).
- **Editor:** freelance video editor (Instagram/Fiverr/Upwork, Bangalore-based ideally), ₹2,500–5,000 per long video including 3 Reels cut-downs.
- **Thumbnails:** your face + one big number ("₹13 Cr", "Day 1"), consistent template.
- **Every video ends with:** "Own land near Bangalore? Get a free Land Potential Snapshot. Link in description / WhatsApp [number]."

## 5. Occupier content (LinkedIn company page + email, monthly)

- **"Bangalore Warehouse Monitor"**: a one-page monthly note: new leases (from CRE Matrix/Propstack/news), rents by corridor, new supply, what it means. Send to every occupier and broker in the CRM. Over 12 months, this alone makes you known.
- **Spec sheets:** "BTS spec menu" PDF: three building tiers with lead times.
- **Site list:** the latest available BTS sites (no landowner names), refreshed monthly.
