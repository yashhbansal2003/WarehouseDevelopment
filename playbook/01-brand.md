# 01 — Brand

> **Note for Yash:** a brand is the *promise people repeat about you when you are not in the room*. The name is only the label on that promise. A good name for us has to work in three rooms: a **Kannada-speaking landowner's living room**, a **3PL real-estate head's inbox**, and a **bank credit committee**.

## 1. First decision: Bansal umbrella or a new name?

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Pure Bansal brand** ("Bansal Warehousing") | Instant family credibility; one brand to build | "Bansal" is common nationally (many unrelated Bansal firms, which hurts Google searches and trademarks); ties the platform's reputation to every family business and the other way round; harder to bring in an outside partner or investor later; sounds like a family firm, not a professional platform, to corporates |
| **B. New name, no link** | Clean and professional; can be sold, partnered or raised on | Starts with zero trust, which is fatal with landowners in year 1 |
| **C. New name + "A Bansal Group company" endorsement** ✅ | New name for corporates/Google/ads; the family name gives landowners and banks trust; you can drop the endorsement later | Two names to explain (a small cost) |

**Recommendation: Option C.** Use a new name, endorsed "A Bansal Group company" on the brochure, site boards, website footer and landowner materials. On LinkedIn ads and corporate decks the new name stands alone.

## 2. Name options

| # | Name | Meaning / positioning | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sthira Developments** (ಸ್ಥಿರ) ⭐ | *Sthira* = stable, permanent. In Kannada, *sthiraasthi* (ಸ್ಥಿರಾಸ್ತಿ) literally means **immovable property / real estate**. Positioning: "stable income from permanent land" | Every Kannada landowner understands it instantly; easy for English speakers too; unusual, so good for Google and trademark; carries "stable rental income" and "your land stays yours" | Non-Kannada corporates won't know the meaning (they don't need to) |
| 2 | **Groundwork Industrial** | "We do the groundwork": approvals, builders, tenant, loan | Very clear in English; good for LinkedIn/corporates | Generic, so harder to trademark and own on Google; means nothing in Kannada |
| 3 | **Bhoomiyield** | *Bhoomi* (land) + yield | Says exactly what we sell | "Bhoomi" is also the name of Karnataka's government land-records system, so it may look official or be confused with it. Avoid |
| 4 | **Landlease Partners** | Descriptive: land → lease | Very clear; SEO-friendly | Sounds like a broker; hard to trademark |
| 5 | **Dockline Developments** | Docks = warehouses; "line" = timeline | Modern and industrial; good for occupiers | Means nothing to landowners |
| 6 | **Anchorpoint Warehousing** | Anchor tenant, anchor asset | Good for tenant-first positioning | Long; "anchor" is a common word |
| 7 | **Kshetra Logistics Parks** | *Kshetra* = field / land (Sanskrit, understood in Kannada) | Rooted and respectful | "Logistics Parks" sounds institutional, bigger than our 50k–3L sq ft sweet spot |
| 8 | **Bansal Build-to-Lease** | Option A done well | Credibility | All the Option A drawbacks |

### Recommendation: **Sthira Developments — A Bansal Group company**

- **Legal name:** Sthira Developments Pvt Ltd (or LLP if your CA prefers)
- **Tagline (English):** *From your land to a leased warehouse.*
- **Tagline (Kannada):** ನಿಮ್ಮ ಭೂಮಿ, ನಿಮ್ಮದೇ. ಬಾಡಿಗೆ ಆದಾಯ, ನಮ್ಮ ಜವಾಬ್ದಾರಿ. *("Your land stays yours. The rental income is our responsibility.")* Have your field BD hire check the tone.
- **Descriptor under the logo:** Warehouse Development · Bengaluru

**Week-1 checks before you commit** (15 minutes each; do them in this order):
1. MCA company name availability (mca.gov.in → "Check company name").
2. Trademark search on ipindia.gov.in, classes **36** (real estate services) and **37** (construction). File your own TM application (~₹4,500–9,000 government fee for a startup/individual) as soon as the name is final.
3. Domains: try `sthira.in`, `sthira.co.in`, `sthiradevelopments.com`, `sthira.build`. Get the `.com` or `.in` that is free; don't pay a premium for a parked domain.
4. Instagram/LinkedIn/YouTube handle: `@sthiradevelopments`.
5. Google the name + "Bangalore" to check no other real-estate firm uses it.

If Sthira fails any check, use **#2 Groundwork Industrial**, with the same endorsement and the same Kannada tagline.

## 3. Brand pillars (what every ad, post and script must prove)

| Pillar | What we say | Proof we show |
|---|---|---|
| **Tenant-first** | "We find the tenant before you spend on construction." | LOI-before-construction rule; leasing head's track record; tenant list |
| **Open-book** | "Every quote in front of you. Our fee is our only income." | Three-tier vendor matrix; sample cost sheets from Hoskote 001 |
| **One team, one contract** | "Approvals, design, construction, loan, tenant, maintenance — one accountable team." | 7-stage process graphic; one named project head per project |
| **Fixed timeline** | "Land-to-lease in a written timeline with penalties on contractors." | Hoskote 001 timeline (plan vs actual, published honestly) |
| **We build on our own land too** | "We are landowners. We built Hoskote with our own family's money." | Hoskote 001 video series |

## 4. Voice

- **To landowners:** respectful, slow, family-to-family. Kannada first. Talk about *monthly income, security for children, the land staying in the family*. Never use the words "yield", "LRD" or "capex" without explaining them.
- **To corporates:** crisp and numbers-first. Specs, timelines, rent, delivery date.
- **Everywhere:** never exaggerate. Use real photos, never stock warehouse images. Show mistakes too (it builds trust).

## 5. Visual identity (brief to a designer)

- **Brief:** "An industrial-grade, trustworthy developer: not flashy, not a broker. Think engineering plus family values."
- **Colours:** deep steel-blue as primary (trust, steel buildings), warm earth/terracotta accent (land, Karnataka soil), off-white background.
- **Logo:** wordmark "STHIRA" with a simple mark (a roof line over a horizon/land line). Must be readable at 32 px (WhatsApp profile) and on a 10 ft site hoarding.
- **Typeface:** a clean sans (e.g., Inter, Manrope) + Noto Sans Kannada for Kannada material.
- **Photography rule:** only real photos from Hoskote 001 and real team members.
- **Deliverables:** logo set, business card, letterhead, PPT template, A4 brochure (English + Kannada), site hoarding (10×20 ft), vehicle sticker, WhatsApp/LinkedIn banners, safety-helmet and jacket print.
- **Who to hire:** a freelance brand designer on Behance or Instagram (search "brand identity designer Bangalore"). Budget ₹60k–1.5 L. Ask to see two real-estate or industrial brand projects first.
