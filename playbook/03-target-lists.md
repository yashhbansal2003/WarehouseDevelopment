### A3. How to build the *local* broker list (the real gold)

The best Hoskote/Nelamangala brokers have no websites. Find them like this, in about 2 hours:
1. Open **99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, NoBroker, IndiaMART and OLX**. Search "warehouse for rent Hoskote", "industrial land Hoskote", "warehouse Nelamangala", "industrial land Dabaspete", "land Doddaballapur".
2. Every **agent name and phone** on those listings is a broker active on our corridors. Put them in a spreadsheet: name, phone, area, number of listings.
3. Sort by number of listings. **The top 30 are your local broker list.** Call 5 a day with the broker script (file 04).
4. Add the brokers that your field BD meets near the sub-registrar offices.

## C. Banks and NBFCs: construction finance and LRD

> **Reality check:** for a ₹12–30 Cr project leased to a strong tenant, **private and PSU banks are often 1–2% cheaper than NBFCs** for LRD. NBFCs are faster and more flexible, especially for construction finance *before* a tenant is signed. Keep both in the matrix and let them compete. Also note that Edelweiss has largely wound down its wholesale real-estate lending, so move it to P3 and check whether it is active before spending time on it.
