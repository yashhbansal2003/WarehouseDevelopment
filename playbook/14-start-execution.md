# 14 — Start Execution: The First 30 Days

> **Rule for the first 30 days:** do things in the order below. Each step unlocks the next: no ads before the website, no website before the name, no case-study numbers before the Hoskote model is fixed. **Work in parallel on two tracks:** the **Setup track** (brand, tools, website) and the **Ground track** (talking to people). Never let setup stop you from talking to people; the conversations are what teach you the market.

---

## The 5 things that matter most this month

1. **Fix the brand name** (week 1), because everything else carries the name.
2. **Rebuild the Hoskote 001 numbers** (week 1), because it's your proof and every pitch uses it.
3. **Talk to 20 landowners and 20 brokers** (weeks 1–4), so you learn faster than any course can teach.
4. **Get the tools live:** WhatsApp Business, CRM, LinkedIn (week 2), then the website (week 4).
5. **Start the Hoskote video series** (week 1). Every week you don't film is footage you never get back.

---

## WEEK 1 — Decide and start talking

### Day 1 (Monday): Name + Hoskote numbers
**Setup track (2–3 hrs)**
- [ ] Check "Sthira Developments" at **mca.gov.in** → MCA Services → "Check Company/LLP Name".
- [ ] Trademark search at **ipindia.gov.in** → Public Search → Wordmark "STHIRA", classes 36 and 37.
- [ ] Domain check on **GoDaddy / Namecheap / BigRock**: `sthira.in`, `sthira.co.in`, `sthiradevelopments.com`.
- [ ] Google "Sthira Bangalore real estate" to make sure nobody close uses it.
- ✅ If all clear → **buy the domains today** (₹1–3k). If not → run the same checks for "Groundwork Industrial".

**Ground track (2 hrs)**

- [ ] Send these to Claude in this project: *"Build the Hoskote 001 financial model."* I'll build the spreadsheet: rent scenarios (₹21 / ₹23.5 / ₹26), loan, 20-year cash flow and real PAT.

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Company + phone + WhatsApp

### Day 3 (Wednesday): LinkedIn + first broker calls
- [ ] **Your LinkedIn profile:** professional photo (ideally at the Hoskote site in a helmet); headline: *"Founder, Sthira Developments | From your land to a leased warehouse | Bangalore"*; About section: 3 short paragraphs (why, what, who it's for); banner image (a Hoskote site photo + the promise). Claude can draft the About text.
- [ ] Create the **LinkedIn company page** (logo can wait; use a site photo).
- [ ] **Build the local broker list** (file 03 §A3): 2 hours on 99acres / MagicBricks / Housing / IndiaMART; capture 50 names in a Google Sheet.
- [ ] **Call 5 brokers** using script 04 §2A. Goal: 1 meeting this week.

### Day 4 (Thursday): Hoskote video Ep. 1 + parcel mapping
- [ ] **Film Episode 1** at the site (file 06 §3): 3–5 minutes of you talking about why you're building it, plus 2 minutes of walking footage on your phone. Don't wait for perfect gear.
- [ ] Install the **Dishank** app. Starting from Hoskote 001, mark every parcel ≥3 acres within 3 km of NH-75 that looks undeveloped. **Target: 15 parcels** in a Google Sheet (survey no., village, approx. acres, road access, notes).

### Day 5 (Friday): Hiring + designer
- [ ] **Post the Field BD job** (file 11 §A) on Apna, Naukri, LinkedIn and local WhatsApp groups. Ask every broker and document writer you meet: *"Who is the most trusted young person in this area who knows land?"*
- [ ] **Start the Leasing Head search:** send 10 LinkedIn messages to Industrial & Logistics people at the IPCs and developers (file 11 §B).
- [ ] **Brief a brand designer** (file 01 §5). Find 3 on Behance/Instagram, ask for quotes and portfolios, and pick one by Monday.

### Day 6 (Saturday): Field day
- [ ] Spend the morning at the **Hoskote Sub-Registrar Office** area. Meet 3 document writers and 2 stamp vendors; introduce yourself; leave your number; mention the referral fee.
- [ ] Spend the afternoon visiting 3–5 of the mapped parcels. Talk to anyone around (neighbours, farmers, shopkeepers) and **just listen**: who owns what, who wants to sell, who's been approached by builders.
- [ ] Write down every phrase people use (for ads later).

### Day 7 (Sunday): Review + learn
- [ ] Learning Module 1: *Obviously Awesome*, chapters 1–3.
- [ ] Write your first LinkedIn post (file 06, week 1: "Why I'm starting this company") and send it to Claude for editing. Post on Tuesday.
- [ ] Fill in the week-1 scorecard: brokers called, landowners met, parcels mapped.
