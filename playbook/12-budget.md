## 3. Expected funnel economics (assumptions to replace with real data by month 4)

| Step | Assumed rate / cost |
|---|---|
| Paid landowner lead (form/WhatsApp) | ₹800–2,500 per lead |
| Lead → qualified (right land, right person) | 20–35% |
| **Cost per qualified paid lead** | ~₹3,000–8,000 |
| Qualified → site visit | 25–40% |
| **Cost per site visit (paid channels)** | ~₹10,000–30,000 |
| Site visit → paid feasibility | ~50% |
| Feasibility → mandate | ~35–50% |
| **Paid-media cost per mandate** | ~₹1–3 L, against a fee of ₹45–95 L |

Even if the real numbers are 3× worse than these assumptions, one mandate pays for the full year's marketing. **The risk is not overspending; it is spending without tracking.**

## 4. A note on Year-1 economics (honest view)

With a ₹1.5 Cr/yr team, ₹30 L marketing and office/overheads, Year-1 costs are roughly **₹2–2.3 Cr**. Mandates signed in months 5–11 only pay part of their fee in Year 1 (milestones run over 12–18 months). A realistic Year-1 fee income is **₹70 L–1.2 Cr** (feasibility fees + first fee milestones + any Hoskote-related income). **Plan Year 1 as an investment year with a likely operating loss**, and model whether the ₹1–1.5 Cr startup capital covers it. If it's tight, hire the leasing head on a lower fixed salary with higher incentives, and delay the second site engineer until a client project starts construction.
